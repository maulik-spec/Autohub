Our concept

Cars deserve a closer look.

AutoHub is a car showcase website concept developed as a BCA Semester 1 web development project. It presents car information in a clean, structured and visually engaging format.

The website focuses on front-end fundamentals: semantic HTML, CSS layouts, responsive design, reusable components, tables, forms and hover transitions.
